The bigger picture

Why water matters

Water gives us life. We drink it, cook with it, grow food with it, and share it with animals, fish and plants. It is also far more limited than most of us think.

If Earth’s water were 100 drops

  • 97 drops are salty ocean water.
  • 2 drops are frozen in ice.
  • Less than 1 drop is fresh and clean enough for us to use. This is the drop we all share.
700 million+people around the world do not have safe, clean water close to home.
6 litresof water run out of a tap every minute while you brush your teeth.
11,000 Lcan be lost in a year to a tap that drips once every second.
45 litresis about what a 5-minute shower uses. Every minute you cut saves roughly 9.

What is SDG 6?

Sustainable Development Goal 6 is the United Nations promise of clean water and sanitation for everyone. It asks countries, businesses and families to use water wisely, keep it clean and share it fairly.

Water Guardians turns that promise into stories, games and tips anyone can use today.

What can I do?

You do not need to wait for anyone else. Turn off a tap, fix a drip, collect rainwater, plant a tree, or ask your school or workplace how it uses water. Small actions add up quickly when many people do them together.

At home

3 things you can do to save water

  • 1

    Shower for 4 minutes or less. Set a timer or sing one short song, and when it is done, turn off the tap. A shorter shower saves up to 30 litres every single day.

  • 2

    Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth. A running tap uses about 6 litres per minute. Wet your brush, turn it off, brush for 2 minutes, rinse quickly.

  • 3

    Collect rainwater for plants. Put a bucket or barrel outside when it rains and use that water on the garden instead of the tap. Your plants will love it too.
